Why me.

I have never held the title Product Manager, and I have not worked in a contact center. I have spent twenty years taking client work from scope to launch, and this year I have built and run AI agents of my own.

The gap, plainly

I have not worked in a contact center.

I have learned a new client’s operation many times, and I would expect to learn this one the same way: in the room with the people who run it. This site is the reading half of that, done in a day.

My titles have been Producer, Scrum Master, Agile Coach and Release Train Engineer. I am the product owner and builder of the tools named above.

I do know voice. At Meta I supported the embedded voice in the Ray-Ban Stories glasses and ran final QA. It shipped on the public launch date.
