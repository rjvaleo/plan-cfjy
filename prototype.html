      What this replaces

      I do not know what Cresta shows a customer’s executives on decision day. Its public pages describe release criteria and thresholds, and the testing underneath is thorough.

      I found no public picture of the decision itself. If Cresta has one, this is my version of it, and I would use theirs.

      Choices I made in this page

      • No data shows a dash.A green light nobody checked is worse than no light. I carry this rule from the planning board I built for my current program.
      • A grader nobody has checked counts as no data.Nine passes out of nine still show a dash until the lender’s reviewers have checked the grader.
      • The failing turn is two clicks away.An executive should see what failed, not only that something did.
      • The decision is per use case.Four can launch while three wait, and the date holds.
      • A person’s entry is marked as a person’s entry.Every line says where its answer came from.
