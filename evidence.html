What changed in this market in 2026

Four changes that shape how an agent gets deployed now, and one thing that has not changed.

  • There is now an independent standard for AI agents.AIUC-1 pairs an audit with adversarial tests on the live agent. Sierra announced it on September 17, 2026, and Cresta on November 4. Sierra, Cresta
  • Testing moved from scripts to simulated customers.Five of the six rivals describe it, and three vendors including Cresta now simulate bad audio as well. Links are in the table above.
  • Forward-deployed tools are being handed to customers.On October 29, 2026 Cresta opened the tools its own teams use to customer and partner developers. A playbook now has to work when the customer runs it. Self-serve model
  • Some callers are now AI agents too.Cresta reports a sharp rise in personal AI agents contacting its customers, and released a model to detect them. That is a new group of test conversations. Sentry, October 12, 2026
  • The decision itself is not shown.All seven vendors describe testing and release controls of some kind. On their public pages I found no go or no-go view for the customer’s executives. That is why I built one.
